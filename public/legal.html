How SHIOL+ works

Clear information about our statistical analysis, privacy, and responsible use.

About the Project

SHIOL+ is a statistical analysis and mathematical research platform. It compares independently generated probabilistic combinations against official public draw results to explore patterns and interpret the performance of algorithmic models.

All reports are strictly descriptive and informational.

Privacy Policy

SHIOL+ public reports are designed to be accessed without registration or a user account. We do not collect lottery purchase data, financial details, or sensitive personal information.

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

Terms of Use

By using SHIOL+, you agree to use the platform solely for personal, informational, and entertainment purposes. It is your responsibility to verify the applicable laws in your jurisdiction.

Cookie Policy

SHIOL+ v9 does not use persistent tracking cookies or commercial tracking technologies. Report data is fetched directly from the service on each visit.

Important Disclaimer

SHIOL+ is a product intended solely for statistical, educational, and entertainment purposes. No model, score, algorithm, or past or projected combination guarantees a prize or predicts the outcome of any future draw.

Playing the lottery involves financial risk. SHIOL+ does not constitute financial advice or a recommendation to play.
