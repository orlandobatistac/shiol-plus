How SHIOL+ works

Clear information about our analysis, your privacy, and responsible use.

About

SHIOL+ is an entertainment and statistical analysis product. It compares independently generated lottery strategy combinations with published draw results so visitors can explore historical patterns and model performance.

Reports are descriptive, not recommendations or promises.

Privacy Policy

SHIOL+ reports are designed to be readable without an account. We do not ask for lottery purchase details, payment information, or sensitive personal data in the public report experience.

Operational data may be processed to keep the service secure, available, and accurate. We do not sell personal information.

Terms of Use

By using SHIOL+, you agree to use the reports lawfully and for personal, informational, and entertainment purposes. You are responsible for checking the rules, age requirements, and availability of any lottery in your jurisdiction.

Content may change as data sources and analysis methods are updated.

Cookie Policy

The current v9 public report experience does not set cookies or use local browser storage, IndexedDB, or similar persistent tracking technologies. The interface loads its report data from the service for each visit.

If this changes, this policy will be updated before the new use is introduced.

Important Disclaimer

SHIOL+ is for entertainment and statistical analysis only. No strategy, score, ranking, historical result, or combination guarantees a prize or predicts a future draw.

Lottery play involves risk. Follow the legal age and responsible-play rules where you live, set limits, and do not spend money you cannot afford to lose. SHIOL+ is not financial advice, gambling advice, or a substitute for official lottery information.
